THE THEOREM BEING CHECKED

Every nontrivial zeta zero has real part ½.

ζ(s) = 0, s ∉ {−2, −4, −6, …}, s ≠ 1 ⇒ Re(s) = ½

Here s is a complex number. The negative even integers are the trivial zeros; s = 1 is excluded. The claim concerns every other zero, at any height.

Exact Lean statement
theorem riemannHypothesis : RiemannHypothesis := rhOfAll

-- RiemannHypothesis in the pinned mathlib:
∀ (s : ℂ) (_ : riemannZeta s = 0)
  (_ : ¬∃ n : ℕ, s = -2 * (n + 1)) (_ : s ≠ 1),
  s.re = 1 / 2
